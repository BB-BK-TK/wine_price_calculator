THE IDEA BEHIND IT

Can data predict a great vintage?

This prototype started with economist Orley Ashenfelter’s research on Bordeaux wines. His model found relationships between auction prices, vintage age, and weather conditions such as winter rain, growing-season temperature, and harvest rain.

Our newer model asks a different question: what are comparable wines worth in the market, and what makes this bottle different?

Ash Model is a relative signal. Our Model Beta is an experimental fair-value estimate, not a live retail quote, auction result, production cost, or universal “true price.”
